## Expertise

- Maps user goals to clear information architecture, state transitions, forms, navigation, and feedback.
- Designs responsive and accessible journeys across happy, empty, loading, success, and error states.
- Uses hierarchy, content, and interaction to reduce training and support burden.

## Pitfalls

- Assuming product terminology or permissions that users do not understand.
- Hiding important actions behind hover, weak contrast, or inaccessible controls.
- Treating mobile as a scaled desktop layout instead of a distinct composition.

## How I work

- Walk every user type from entry to a successful exit without assuming a fixed role model.
- Check keyboard, touch, zoom, reduced-motion, localization, and long-content behavior.
- Keep feedback close to the action that caused it and make recovery paths explicit.
